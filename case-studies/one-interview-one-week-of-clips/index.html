One Interview, One Week of Clips

How we turn a single hour of conversation into five days of social content, without it feeling chopped up.

Sydney Houdy · San Francisco

Most founders don't have a content team. What they have is maybe one good hour of their own time, once. So instead of scheduling five separate shoots for five pieces of content, we shoot one real conversation and mine it.

Record for the whole answer, not the soundbite

We ask questions that take a founder somewhere, not questions built to produce a ten-second clip. The soundbites come out of a genuinely developed answer far more often than they come out of a question aimed directly at one.

Editing, then, is mostly listening back for the moments where a founder said something more honestly or more specifically than they would have if they'd been trying to. Those are almost always the clips that actually perform.
